Backup cards

Blank cards for a 12-word seed, to print and fill in by hand while Goldfish shows it. Print them blank: never type words or fill in a QR on a computer.

Format
Text

Print at 100% (no “fit to page”) on A4 or Letter. Fill each square fully with a dark pen; leave the white border around the grid empty. Then choose “Check card” on Goldfish and scan it.
