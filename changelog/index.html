Say hello to Dove

Goldfish gets a wallet app of its own

Dove watches your balance, builds a transaction and shows it as a QR code. Goldfish signs it, and Dove reads it back through your webcam and sends it. Early days: Linux only, not for real funds yet.

Too neat to be real dice

Counted 1-2-3-4-5-6 instead of rolling? Goldfish spots rolls that look made up and asks if you really rolled them.

A QR your webcam can read

Tap the signed QR to fill the screen and turn the brightness right down. Webcams read it even in a dim room.

Bigger transactions

Pay lots of people in one go: up to 16 outputs and 16 KB. Goldfish flags a fee that looks too high.

Goldfish

  • Clear refusals. If it can't sign, Goldfish says why and what to change.
  • Key bubbles. See the key under your finger as you type.
  • One scan screen for a SeedQR or written words.
  • Short words like "day" are read properly.
  • Boxes that fit. Words found boxes whole words, faint first and last letters too, and more words are read.

Dove

  • Backup step for your multisig setup in the first-use wizard.
  • Update Goldfish straight from Dove, checked against the release.
  • Optional password to lock Dove's saved settings.
  • Used before? Review warns when you pay an address you've used or paid before, since that links your payments together.

Site and case

  • Try it live on the home page, in four case colours.
  • Build guide and a steel backup from washers and a bolt.
  • Socket cover for the USB port on the case.
