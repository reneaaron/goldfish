Flash the stateless signer

Writes the firmware to a Waveshare RP2350-Touch-LCD-2 from this page. The firmware works in the desktop simulator and is untested on real hardware: not for real funds.

  1. Hold BOOT on the board and plug in USB-C. A drive called RP2350 appears.
  2. Press the button and choose that drive.
  3. The board restarts into the firmware. Its home screen shows the version in the top right corner: it should match the one below.
