Flash Goldfish

Puts the firmware on a Waveshare RP2350-Touch-LCD-2, and updates it the same way. The firmware works in the desktop simulator and is untested on real hardware: not for real funds.

  1. 1

    Start update mode

    Plug the device into your computer. On its home screen, tap the version number in the top right corner, then Restart. A drive called RP2350 appears on your computer.

    First flash, or the device doesn't start?
    • Take the board out of the case, hold BOOT (right of the USB-C socket, seen from the back) and plug in USB-C.
    • Keep BOOT held until the cable is in.
    • Use a cable that carries data. Some only charge.
    • Unplug and try again: the board only looks at BOOT when it gets power.
  2. 2

    Choose the RP2350 drive

    Your browser asks which folder to open, then whether this page may save to it. Pick the drive itself.

    Download firmware.uf2
  3. 3

    Check the version on the board

    The board restarts by itself. Its home screen shows the version in the top right corner.

    Before you load a seed, unplug the board from the computer and power it from a charger or a power bank.
