Flash Goldfish

Puts the Goldfish firmware on the board, and updates it the same way. The firmware is experimental: not for real funds.

  1. 1

    Start update mode

    New board: hold BOOT (right of the USB-C socket, seen from the back) and plug it into your computer. Let go once the cable is in.

    Already runs Goldfish: plug it in, tap the version number in the top right corner of the home screen, then Restart.

    A drive called RP2350 appears on your computer.

    No drive?
    • Use a cable that carries data. Some only charge.
    • Unplug and try again: the board only looks at BOOT when it gets power.
    • If the board is in its case and won't start, take it out to reach BOOT.
  2. 2

    Choose the RP2350 drive

    Your browser asks which folder to open, then whether this page may save to it. Pick the drive itself.

    Download firmware.uf2
  3. 3

    Check the version on the board

    The board restarts by itself. Its home screen shows the version in the top right corner.

    Before you load a seed, unplug the board from the computer and power it from a charger or a power bank.

    Next: connect the camera

    Or see how signing works, or practise in your browser first.
