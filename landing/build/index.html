Build a Goldfish

One board with a screen and a camera, one printed case, no soldering. The firmware works in the simulator and is untested on real hardware: not for real funds.

Want to see it first? Try it in your browser.

  1. 1

    Get the parts

    • Waveshare RP2350-Touch-LCD-2-C (SKU 30718). The -C version comes with the OV5640 camera. $19.99 at Waveshare in November 2026.
    • A USB-C cable that carries data, not only charge.
    • Access to a 3D printer, for the case.
    Buy from Waveshare Other shops by region
    Only found the board without camera?

    The base board is SKU 30711. Add an OV5640 module with a 24-pin 0.5 mm DVP connector (ESP32-CAM style), and check its pinout against the board's J1 before plugging it in. A 60–75° lens reads QR codes better than a wide-angle one.

  2. 2

    Print the case

    One part, no screws. Print it on an FDM printer, flat on its back, without supports. PLA works.

    Download case.stl
    Change the case

    It's modelled in gcad; the source, a STEP file and the measurements it's built from are in case/.

  3. 3

    Flash the firmware

    Do this before the board goes into the case: the first time, you need the BOOT button, and the case covers it. Hold BOOT (right of the USB-C socket, seen from the back), plug the board into your computer, then let the flasher copy the firmware onto the drive that appears. It needs Chrome or Edge on a computer.

    Open the flasher

    Later updates don't need the button: tap the version on the home screen, then Restart.

  4. 4

    Put it together

    • The camera sits flat on the back of the board, on the microSD slot, lens facing away from the screen. The hole in the case is made for it there.
    • Lower the board into the case, screen up, USB-C socket into the tunnel in the end wall.
    • Press down until the four standoffs sit in the sockets in the posts and the glass is flush with the rim. Ribs in the sockets hold them; no screws.
  5. 5

    Start using it

    Power it from a charger or a power bank, not your computer. Nothing is saved on the device: each time you plug it in, load your seed again.

    • Load a seed: Scan QR on a SeedQR, Enter words, or make a new one from dice, coins or drawn words.
    • Back it up on a paper QR card. Print blank cards for 12-word seeds.
    • Connect wallet shows your public key as a QR for a watch-only wallet. Signing hasn't been tried with Sparrow or another wallet app yet.

    The full list of what it does is in the firmware README.
