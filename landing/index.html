Less to trust

Nothing is saved

The seed lives in memory only. The firmware never writes to storage, so an unplugged device holds nothing.

No Wi-Fi, no Bluetooth

The board has no radio hardware at all. Power it from a charger or a power bank.

QR codes in, QR codes out

Seeds, transactions and addresses come in through the camera. Signatures leave on the screen.

You are the random number generator

The firmware has none. A new seed is made only from what you put in.

  • Roll a die 50 times for 12 words.
  • Flip a coin 128 times.
  • Draw 11 words yourself. The last one comes from coin flips.

Back it up with a pen

Write down the words, or copy the seed onto a paper QR card. The device shows the grid part by part, then scans your card to check you got every square right.

Also in there

Verify an address

Scan a receive address to confirm it belongs to your seed before you share it.

Multisig

Scan your wallet's descriptor, check it on the device, then sign as one of the cosigners.

Child seeds

Derive a BIP-85 child seed and back it up like a new one.

Passphrase

Add a BIP-39 passphrase. Only the derived key is kept, and only until you unplug.

Connect a wallet

Show your xpub as a QR so a wallet app can watch your balance and build transactions.

Native segwit only

Single-sig and sorted multisig, on mainnet and testnet.

Build one

One off-the-shelf board with a touch screen, one camera, one printed case that clips shut. No soldering iron in the parts list.

1

Get the parts

A Waveshare RP2350-Touch-LCD-2 and an OV5640 camera.

 2

Print the case

STL and STEP files, in whatever colour you like.

 3

Flash the firmware

From your browser: hold BOOT, plug in, pick the drive.
