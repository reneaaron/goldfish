The Bitcoin signer that forgets.

An off-the-shelf touch board in a case you print. Scan your seed, sign, unplug. Nothing is saved.

Board in. Case on. That's the hardware.

A Waveshare RP2350 touch board clips into a one-piece printed case. No screws. The board has no Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth hardware.

Your seed, read off paper.

The back camera reads a SeedQR card. Match the fingerprint with your wallet. The seed lives in RAM, never in storage.

Read every line. Then slide to sign.

Scan the transaction QR from your wallet, check amount, fee and each address, and hand back a signed QR. It only signs. Your wallet sends.

Unplug. It forgets.

Pull the cable and the seed is gone. Firmware, case files and parts list are open source.
