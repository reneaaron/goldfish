# Stackbox for 12 washers

`stackbox_12_body.stl` and `stackbox_12_lid.stl` are the
[Knurled Blockmit Stackbox](https://makerworld.com/en/models/196485) by
Printer_Gobrrr, with the body shortened from 54 to 45 mm for 12 M8 × 24 washers
on an M8 × 35 bolt (`hardware/stackbox_12.scad`). The lid is unchanged.
