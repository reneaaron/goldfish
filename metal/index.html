Back up in steel

Twelve steel washers, one word hammered into each, on a bolt. Stainless steel melts at about 1,400 °C; paper burns.

You need

  • 12 stainless washers M8 × 24 mm (DIN 9021), an M8 × 35 bolt and a nutabout $5, any hardware store
  • 6 mm letter and number punchesabout $35, once
  • A hammer

About $40 in all. Each further backup: about $5.

Optional, printed

  • A stamping jig holds the washer and lines up the letters. Parametric Washer Stamping Jig (made for M8 × 24 washers and 6 mm punches)Print it solid: it takes hammer blows
  • A screw-top box that fits the 12-washer bolt: body and lid. Shortened from Printer_Gobrrr's Knurled Blockmit Stackbox (CC BY-NC-SA)Fine layers (0.15 mm or less), 4 walls. Plastic hides it; the steel is what survives a fire

Do

  1. Stamp each word's number and first four letters: 01 UNVE for unveil.
  2. Put the washers on the bolt in order. Nut on, tight.
  3. Check: on Goldfish tap Enter words and type the letters from each washer.

Ask for stainless A4 or A2, not zinc-plated. Practise on a spare washer first.
