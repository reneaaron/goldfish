Get the parts

Two things to buy and one to print. Nothing is soldered, and the case needs no screws.

  • Buy

    Waveshare RP2350-Touch-LCD-2-C

    SKU 30718. The board with its 2" touch screen, and an OV5640 camera in the same box. The -C at the end is what gets you the camera.

  • Buy, or find in a drawer

    USB-C data cable

    Powers the device and flashes the firmware. A charge-only cable powers it but can't flash it.

  • Print

    The case

    One piece, about 37 × 59 × 17 mm. Files and settings below.

If you can only get the board without a camera

The base board (SKU 30711) works with an OV5640 module bought separately. The module must have the 24-pin, 0.5 mm DVP ribbon (the kind sold for ESP32-CAM boards). Check its pinout against the board's camera connector before plugging it in. A 60–75° lens suits scanning QR codes better than a wide-angle one.

Print the case

  • Flat on its back, no supports.
  • PLA works, in whatever colour you like.
  • The long walls are only 0.75 mm thick, so handle the case gently.
  • The board's four standoffs press into the case. How tightly they grip depends on your printer.
