First use

Give Goldfish a seed, connect it to a wallet app, then receive a small amount and send it out again. Goldfish holds the key and signs. The wallet app shows your balance and builds the transactions.

Goldfish is early software. Try it with test coins, not real funds.

  1. Set up a seed

    Plug the device into a charger or a power bank. The home screen offers three ways in.

    You already have a seed

    Tap Scan QR to scan its SeedQR, or Enter words to type it in.

    You need a new one

    1. Tap Create new seed and make it from dice rolls, coin flips or drawn words.
    2. Write the words down as the device shows them, for example on the backup cards. This is the only copy: nothing is saved on the device.
    3. Then load the seed from what you wrote, with Scan QR or Enter words. Creating a seed doesn't load it. Entering it again is how you find out that your backup is right.

    With a seed loaded, the screen says Ready to sign and shows the seed's fingerprint. Unplugging forgets the seed, so you load it from your backup each time you use the device.

  2. Show your public key

    On Goldfish, tap Wallet options, then Connect wallet. The Watch-only wallet screen shows a QR code with your public key. It lets a wallet app see your balance and prepare transactions, but not spend.

    For test coins, set Network in Wallet options to testnet first.

  3. Scan it with a wallet app

    Goldfish works together with a wallet app, sometimes called a coordinator. This guide uses Sparrow Wallet on a computer with a webcam. There are others, some of them for phones.

    1. In Sparrow, create a new wallet.
    2. Choose watch-only wallet.
    3. Press the camera button and hold the Goldfish screen up to your webcam.
    4. Check that the fingerprint in Sparrow is the one on the Goldfish screen.

  4. Receive a test amount

    Your new wallet can receive. Before you use an address, let Goldfish confirm that it belongs to your seed.

    1. In Sparrow, open a receive address. There is a QR code next to it.
    2. On Goldfish, tap Scan QR and scan that code. Address verified means the address belongs to your wallet.
    3. From another wallet, send a small test amount to the address, ideally 1,000 to 10,000 sats.

  5. Send some out again

    Sending takes two scans: the unsigned transaction goes from Sparrow to Goldfish, and the signed one comes back.

    From Sparrow to Goldfish

    1. In Sparrow, paste the address you want to send to and prepare the transaction.
    2. Press Show QR and choose low density. That makes an animated QR code that Goldfish can scan more easily.
    3. On Goldfish, tap Scan QR and hold it up to the code until it has all the parts.

    Review and sign on Goldfish

    1. Check the amount and the fee, then Check addresses to see where each part goes.
    2. Slide to sign. This only signs: your wallet app sends the transaction.

    From Goldfish back to Sparrow

    1. Goldfish now shows the signed transaction as an animated QR code.
    2. In Sparrow, click the scan button and hold the Goldfish screen up to your webcam.
    3. Sparrow now has the signed transaction and can send it.

That was the whole loop. See everything else the device does.
