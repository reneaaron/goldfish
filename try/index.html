Try Goldfish in your browser

This is the device's own firmware, built for the browser instead of the board: the same screens and the same signing code. Tap the screen to use it. In place of the camera, pick what it sees: a test picture, your webcam, or a picture of your own.

Demo onlyA browser tab is not a device that forgets. Use the test seed here; never type or scan a seed that holds real funds.

Things to try

  1. Tap Scan QR and pick Test seed. The device shows the seed's fingerprint.
  2. Tap Scan QR again and pick Transaction. Check the amounts and addresses, slide to sign, and the signed transaction comes out as a QR.
  3. Under Wallet options, show the xpub for a watch-only wallet.
  4. Start over and Create new seed from dice or coin flips, or Enter words and scan the Handwritten backup with the camera button.
