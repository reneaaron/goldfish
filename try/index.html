Try Hermitkey in your browser

This is the device's own firmware, built for the browser instead of the board: the same screens, the same signing code. Tap the screen to use it. Instead of a camera, pick what it should see from the pictures on the right, use your webcam, or open a picture.

Demo only. A browser tab is not a stateless device. Use the test seed here; never type or scan a seed that holds real funds.

What the camera sees

Used whenever a scan screen is open.

Things to try

  1. Tap Scan QR and pick Test seed. The device shows the seed's fingerprint.
  2. Tap Scan QR again and pick Transaction to sign. Check the amounts and addresses, swipe to sign, and the device shows the signed transaction as a QR.
  3. Under Wallet options, show the xpub for a watch-only wallet.
  4. Wipe it, then Create new seed from dice rolls or coin flips, or Enter words and scan the Handwritten backup.

Nothing leaves this page: it makes no requests after loading, and the seed is gone when you close the tab. The firmware's source says what the device does.
